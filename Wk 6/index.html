Momma Don’t Cry No’Mo Smash Burgers

This recipe was born from love—something to make my momma smile. She is a single parent who never once let me fall, even when life tried to knock us both down.

She loves onions, bacon, and a big ol’ burger, so that is exactly what she is getting.

What You’ll Need

  • 1 red onion
  • 1.5 lb ground beef, using your preferred blend
  • 2 sweet onions
  • 2.5 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 1.25 tablespoons brown sugar
  • Half a pack of bacon
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons spicy mustard
  • A splash of pickle juice
  • 1 pickle, sliced into chips
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 types of cheese, your choice
  • Burger buns

Bacon Onion Jam

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and cook the bacon for 15 to 20 minutes.
  2. Chop the sweet onions into small cubes.
  3. Add butter to a hot pan, then sauté the onions for 15 to 30 minutes until soft and golden.
  4. Add the balsamic vinegar and cooked bacon.
  5. Gently stir in the brown sugar until it melts into a sweet and savory bacon onion jam.

The Smash Burgers

  1. While the jam cooks, divide the ground beef into smash patties that are about 1/4 lb each.
  2. Place long slices of red onion on a very hot pan or griddle.
  3. Put a patty over the onions and smash it down with a burger press or spatula.
  4. Cook the first side for 2 to 2 1/2 minutes, until the edges are crispy and lacy.
  5. Flip the burger, add cheese, and cook for another 45 seconds.
  6. Top each patty with a large dollop of bacon onion jam over the melted cheese.
Get the pan or griddle aggressively hot. That heat helps the burger grab onto the onions and form a crispy smash-burger crust.

Momma Sauce

Mix the following ingredients together in a small bowl:

  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup
  • Spicy mustard
  • A splash of pickle juice

Spread the Momma Sauce on the bottom bun and add the pickle chips.

Serve With Love

Build each burger on a bun with Momma Sauce, pickle chips, cheesy smash patties, and bacon onion jam. Serve with fries, tater tots, chips, or whatever makes your heart happy.

Hand one to your momma and enjoy a gourmet smash burger made with real love.

“Don’t cry no’mo, Momma—dinner’s served.”
